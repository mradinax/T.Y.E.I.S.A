# TYESIA Voice Commands Guide

## Wake Word
Use **"TYESIA"** to activate the assistant. You can say:
- "TYESIA [command]"
- "Hey TYESIA [command]"  
- "Activate TYESIA [command]"
- "OK TYESIA [command]"

## Available Voice Commands

### 🎵 YouTube Commands
- **"TYESIA open YouTube and play Doraemon"**
- **"TYESIA play Doraemon on YouTube"**
- **"TYESIA YouTube play Iron Man soundtrack"**

### 📱 Open Apps
- **"TYESIA open Spotify"**
- **"TYESIA open Instagram"**
- **"TYESIA open Netflix"**
- **"TYESIA open WhatsApp"**
- **"TYESIA launch Gmail"**
- **"TYESIA start Camera"**

*Supported apps: Spotify, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Netflix, YouTube, Gmail, Chrome, TikTok, Discord, Camera, Calculator, Settings, and many more*

### 🎶 Music Player Commands
- **"TYESIA play Bohemian Rhapsody on Spotify"**
- **"TYESIA play Shape of You on YouTube Music"**
- **"TYESIA search Imagine Dragons on Apple Music"**
- **"TYESIA play Ed Sheeran on SoundCloud"**

*Supported players: Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Deezer, Amazon Music, Pandora*

### 🎵 Spotify Specific
- **"TYESIA play Taylor Swift on Spotify"**
- **"TYESIA Spotify search Coldplay"**
- **"TYESIA search The Weeknd Spotify"**

### 📞 Phone Calls
- **"TYESIA call +1234567890"**
- **"TYESIA phone 555-123-4567"**
- **"TYESIA dial (555) 123-4567"**

*Note: Phone permission will be requested on first use*

## How It Works

1. **Say the wake word**: Start with "TYESIA" to activate
2. **Voice recognition**: The app listens for 15 seconds to capture your complete sentence
3. **Command processing**: TYESIA checks if it's an action command (open app, play music, etc.)
4. **Execute or chat**: 
   - If it's a command → TYESIA executes it directly
   - If it's a question/conversation → TYESIA uses AI to respond

```
🗣️ "TYESIA open YouTube and play Doraemon"

🗣️ "TYESIA open Instagram" 
🤖 "Opening Instagram"

🗣️ "TYESIA play Bohemian Rhapsody on Spotify"
🤖 "Playing Bohemian Rhapsody on Spotify"
🎵 Spotify opens with the song search

```

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Speak clearly** and at normal pace
2. **Use the exact app names** (Spotify, Instagram, etc.)
3. **Be specific** with song/artist names for music commands
4. **Wait for TYESIA to respond** before giving the next command
5. **Phone commands need permission** - grant when prompted

## Command Priority

TYESIA processes commands in this order:
1. **App opening** commands (open, launch, start)
2. **YouTube** commands (play on YouTube)
3. **Music player** commands (play on Spotify/etc.)
4. **Phone call** commands
5. **General chat** (everything else goes to AI)

This means specific action commands are executed immediately, while questions and conversations are handled by the AI chat system.
